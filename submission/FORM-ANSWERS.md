**What works right now?**
```
A browser survival game set in our school where the building is the enemy: three
times a round a disaster tears part of it apart — a meteor takes the library roof,
an earthquake throws everyone around and brings the ceiling down on anyone still
under a roof, and the flood rises through the quad layer by layer, hurting you from
the first layer. A meteor shower marks and drops visible fireballs on anyone
standing in the open, gold supply drops heal whoever reaches them first, and bots
fill the empty seats so one laptop still has eight players. It runs from one laptop
on the school network — no internet, no installs, no accounts. Bed Wars is the
second mode on the same map: four teams, four beds, a shop, and bots that play the
whole round.
```

for real.
