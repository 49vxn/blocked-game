# Blocked — demo video script

Target **1:50**. Judges get a link, the recording, and three one-liners; the
recording only has to show the game working and be under 2:00.

Read this once, then record. You are not reading it on camera.

---

## The round, exactly (so you shoot the right seconds)

Survival is **115 seconds**. Three disasters in shuffled order, hit at **12s,
42s and 72s**, each with a banner **7 seconds** before. On top of that:

- **Meteor shower** starts at **30s**: two marked rocks every **8 seconds**,
  aimed at people standing in the open. Red rings, then craters.
- **Earthquake** bounces every player for **6 seconds** and caves in the science
  corridor bays.
- **Field flood** starts rising at **78s**, one layer every 3s, then deepens at
  **95s**. Ankle water already costs health.
- **Supply drops** land at **38s** and **105s** on the gold beacon.

That means the good stuff is front-loaded: warning at 5s, first impact at 12s,
shower from 30s. Record one continuous round and cut to the beats you got.
